Wellness Guidance

Positive mood: Continue healthy routines, communication and regular rest.

Neutral mood: Encourage regular check-ins, social connection and relaxation activities.

Low mood: Offer supportive communication and contact authorized mission medical or psychological personnel when needed.

Sleep duration, stress levels and clinical mental-health assessments are not measured by this check-in form.
